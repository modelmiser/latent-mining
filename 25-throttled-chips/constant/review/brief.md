# Review brief: a constant bound for the chip game (experiment 25, follow-up)

New in the working tree (not committed): the folder `25-throttled-chips/constant/` and edits to `25-throttled-chips/README.md` (Short answer; two new rows in Results; the new section "From √h to a constant"; a new bullet in "What to distrust"; Files) and to the top-level `README.md` row for experiment 25. The claim: under the firing rule of `25-throttled-chips/solver-a/proofs.txt` THEOREM 2, every height is at most t/5 + 35.2 at every step against every list, hence 5h - 176 <= G(h) <= 5h - 6. The proof is `25-throttled-chips/constant/proofs.txt` (Lemma 1, Lemma 2, Theorem A, Corollary). It has ONE author and is one day old. Assume it is wrong until you have traced every line. The game is defined in `25-throttled-chips/chain/3-question.txt`.

Verdict for each question: CONFIRMED / REFUTED / UNSUPPORTED, with file:line evidence.

- Q1. The three facts taken from Theorem 2: (a) lags are never negative; (b) eta_x = eta*_x + 2 l_x - l_(x-1) - l_(x+1) with eta*_x = (k_(x-2) + ... + k_(x+2))/5 = (t - e_x)/5, where e_x counts adds outside [x-2, x+2]; (d) if the largest lag M right after an add is at least 6, the vertex fired in that round has lag exactly M. Re-derive each (for (d): why is a vertex of largest lag ready; could the rule fire a ready vertex of SMALLER lag; ties).
- Q2. Lemma 1. Does one add raise Phi_a = sum_z max(l_z - a, 0) by at most 1 (three lags rise by 3/5, 1/5, 1/5)? In a round with M >= a + 1 (a >= 5), does the firing lower Phi_a by exactly 1, so that Phi_a does not increase over the round? In a round with M < a + 1, are all lags below a + 1 afterwards? Is the bookkeeping of "after round t" versus "right after the add" consistent?
- Q3. Lemma 2. Follow the proof: the choice of tau; why Phi_a(after t) <= Phi_a(after tau); why L - a <= Phi_a(after t); why Phi_a(after tau) < |Z|; why removing [y-3, y+3] leaves vertices z whose targets T_z involve only adds outside [y-2, y+2]; why l_z <= T_z; the double count "a chip contributes at most 5 to the sum of 5 T_z over distinct z"; the conclusion e_y(t) > a (L - a - 7). Check the edge cases: tau = 0; tau = t; a not an integer; Z' possibly empty; vertices z counted in Z' being distinct.
- Q4. Theorem A and Corollary. With a = 10: l_y <= 17 + e_y/10; height <= (t - e_y)/5 + 2 (17 + e_y/10) = t/5 + 34 after a round. Check the step for the moment right after an add (35.2), including which t and e_y are used. Does "every list of length at most 5h - 177 loses" follow (heights are integers; the win condition tests heights after both steps of every round), and hence G(h) >= 5h - 176 for every h? Is the upper bound 5h - 6 from `solver-b/proofs.txt` P3 stated with the right range of h?
- Q5. Try to break it. Is there any list (think of chips spread widely, then a burst at one point; or two distant bursts) for which the argument's accounting fails, for instance because lag at y is large while few chips were added far from y? Does the proof silently assume a finite set of vertices, a bounded support, or a particular tie rule?
- Q6. The README's section "From √h to a constant": does every sentence follow from `constant/proofs.txt`? Check "the point fired has that largest lag", "each point contributed less than 1", "Leave out the seven points nearest y", "its target counts only chips added at distance 3 or more from y", "one chip counts towards at most three targets, with total weight 1", "more than a·(L − a − 7) chips", "The two terms in e cancel exactly", and the paragraph about the lag being probably unbounded (is it presented as evidence, and does it match `constant/lagadv2_big.txt` and `constant/replay_n16384.txt`?).
- Q7. Status wording. Do the Short answer ("no list beats the single-point list by more than 170 rounds, whatever h is"; "weaker than the simple bound 2h - 1 until h reaches 59"), the two new Results rows, the new "What to distrust" bullet and the top-level row claim more than is established? Is it clear that the bound has one author, is not machine-checked, and that the program tests exercise the lemmas only on lazy variants of the rule (`constant/test_out2.txt`)?
