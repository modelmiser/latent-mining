### Important Items Not Asked in the Brief

1. **Exact \(G(h)\) for \(h \ge 6\) remains unresolved:** Theorems A and B establish \(5h - 28 \le G(h) \le C(h) \le 5h - 10\), but whether \(G(h) < C(h)\) for \(h \ge 6\) is open. Heuristic hill-climbing search (`tight/out_search_h6.txt`) found no winning lists of length 19 at \(h = 6\) or length 24 at \(h = 7\), but this provides only weak negative evidence as exhaustive search was not run.
2. **Slack in the height bound:** The proved height upper bound after an add is \(t/5 + 5.6\) (yielding constant 28), but across 89,243 simulation plays the maximum observed height excess after an add was \(t/5 + 3.2\) (`tight/out_rule_main.txt:7`). The theoretical gap arises primarily because inequality (b) discards the negative discrete Laplacian neighbour lags \(-l_{y-1} - l_{y+1}\).
3. **Failure of Theorem A at \(h = 3\):** Case (II) of Theorem A strictly requires \(m - 2 \ge 4\) (which holds for \(h \ge 4\) where \(m \ge 10\)). For \(h = 3\), \(m = 5\), so \(m - 2 = 3\), allowing round \(m - 2\) to coincide with the opening pass, which is why the argument does not imply \(C(3) \le 5\) (indeed \(C(3) = 7\)).
4. **Arbitrary tie-breaking:** Theorem B applies to Firer play under any tie-breaking rule (even with knowledge of future adds), because Lemma B1 guarantees that whenever \(M \ge 6/5\) some vertex of lag \(M\) is ready, ensuring that Firer always fires a vertex of lag exactly \(M\).
