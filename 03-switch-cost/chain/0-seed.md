# Seed

A single processor serves jobs that arrive online. Time is discrete. Each job arrives at some tick with an integer length (ticks of work it needs) and an integer deadline. An adversary chooses the arrivals, seeing the schedule so far, but may only issue job sets that a clairvoyant scheduler could finish on time if switching between jobs were free. The online scheduler may preempt a job and resume it later, but every time it switches from one unfinished job to a different job the processor is idle for c ticks first (a context switch). Starting a job after another has finished is free. The online scheduler is given a processor that is s times faster than the clairvoyant one. For each switch cost c: what is the smallest speed s(c) with which some online scheduler meets every deadline against every adversary? How does it grow with c, and what does the best scheduler look like (when is it right to refuse to preempt)?
