Hand proof ADV(P) requires \(T = (P-1)(4P-1) \sim 4P^2\), computation up to \(P=5\) suggests \((P-1)(2P+3) \sim 2P^2\), while exact small cases give 4, 9, \(\le 15\) (consistent with \(P^2\)).
3. **Arbitrary Switch Cost \(c > 1\)**: The entire codebase fixes switch penalty to 1 tick. Whether the same-cost game value scales as \(\min(c, P - 1)\) when switch penalty \(c \ge 2\) remains untested.
