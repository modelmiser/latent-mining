### Important items not covered by the questions (most severe first):

1. **Validity of the Shadow Transfer (`FO/Full.lean:248-330`):** The formal theorem relies on `FO.transfer`, establishing that playing the normal-form strategy against a shadow forest bounds the real game value via `g ≤ value k A + flips L A`. If this induction had a flaw, the connection between the normal-form strategy and arbitrary replies in `Spec.lean` would break.
2. **Formal equivalence of positional minimax vs history-dependent strategies:** `Spec.lean` defines `value` purely by backward induction over positions. While standard game theory guarantees equivalence for finite extensive-form games of perfect information, this equivalence is not formalized in Lean.
3. **Reachable forest invariants:** `Pos n := List (Arc n)` technically permits arbitrary lists with cycles or duplicate edges, but proofs rely on `Forest D` and `Valid D` invariants maintained inductively from `[]`.
4. **Compiler warnings during build:** `FO/Normal.lean` (lines 35, 78, 88) and `FO/Reply.lean` (lines 138, 139) trigger unused variable (`hN`, `c`, `o`) and unused `simp` argument warnings during compilation.
