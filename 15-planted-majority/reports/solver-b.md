## 4. Proofs (`proofs.txt`)
- L1 COMPLETE: WLOG one outcome per unordered pair, no swaps.
- L2 COMPLETE: G non-decreasing.
- **T1 COMPLETE.** W = positive integers, K = max W, such that every difference x−y (x>y in W) is in W or a sum of two elements, and every element ≥ 2 is a sum of two elements. States P_w (output A), N_w (output B), wa, wb; qA=P_K, qB=N_K. Rules: {P_x,N_y} → two agents carrying x−y with its sign (weak partner if one suffices; tie → wa,wb); {P_x,wb} → {P_x1,P_x2} with x1+x2=x; mirror for {N_x,wa}. Signed weight Σ is conserved; (Σ|weights|, #weak) drops lexicographically on every move, so no cycles. If a−b ≥ 2, Σ ≥ 2K−K = K; a terminal configuration has a P, hence no N, and if a wb remained then at most a+b agents are P's, all of weight 1, so Σ ≤ a+b < K. So C(W) wins all a+b ≤ K−1. W_m = {1..m−1} ∪ {m,2m,…,m²} gives s=4m, N=m²−1.
- T2 COMPLETE: budget protocol wins a+b ≤ K−2.
- T3 COMPLETE but partial: a protocol winning all N must have only finite plays in every sub-population. Does not decide finiteness of G(s).
- No upper-bound proof for general s. None claimed.
