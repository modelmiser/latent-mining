Q1: CONFIRMED — The scheduler must pick a legal move whenever one exists and is otherwise unrestricted, so it is never told which pair to play (`chain/3-question.txt:5`). A move on an ordered pair is legal exactly when `d(p,q) ≠ (p,q)`, independent of every other agent (`chain/3-question.txt:5`). `R` is the set of configurations reachable from the initial one by legal moves, with an edge for each legal move (`chain/3-question.txt:5`), so any finite continuation of legal moves from a configuration in `R` stays in `R`. — none

Q2: CONFIRMED — Each chosen pair lies in `S_j` and is legal from those two states alone, hence legal in the whole population (`step4-rewritten.md:28-32`, `chain/3-question.txt:5`). Only agents in `S_j` are selected, so each `h_j` is still in its initial state when it is added (`step4-rewritten.md:32-33`). Phase `j` is a sequence of legal moves from the pure population `S_j`, which Step 2 makes finite (`step4-rewritten.md:33-36`, `solver-a/proofs.txt:92-93`). The last phase has `S_{2m+1}` equal to all `2k+2m+1` agents, so “no legal move inside `S`” is “no legal move anywhere” and the sequence is a complete play (`step4-rewritten.md:21-22`, `36-38`). — none

Q3: CONFIRMED — Additions are `q_B`, then pairs `q_A,q_A`, `q_B,q_B`, … (`step4-rewritten.md:22-24`), so from `α_0−β_0 = 0` the differences are `0,−1,0,+1` repeating (`step4-rewritten.md:40-41`). For `j ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, `α=β−1`, so `(α−1, β, plant q_A)` has `a=b−2`, majority B, and initial multiset `α` in `q_A` and `β` in `q_B`; for `j ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, `(α, β−1, plant q_B)` has majority A and matches the same way (`step4-rewritten.md:43-45`). Both have `|a−b|=2`, `a+b=2k+j−1≥2`, `a,b≥0`, and the plant in `Q` (`step4-rewritten.md:46-47`, `chain/3-question.txt:1-3`). Moves up through phase `j` touch only `S_j` and start from that multiset, and phase `j` stops with no legal pair, so `C_j` is reachable and terminal in `I_j`; a win makes every terminal configuration in `R` good (`step4-rewritten.md:47-50`, `chain/3-question.txt:7`). — none

Q4: CONFIRMED — Odd `j` and `j+2` are `1` and `3` mod 4, so all `2k+j` agents of `S_j` output opposite majorities in `C_j` and `C_{j+2}` and therefore change state (`step4-rewritten.md:43-45`, `52-53`). At least `2k+1` agents change and a move changes at most two, so each stretch has at least `k+1` moves (`step4-rewritten.md:53-54`).
