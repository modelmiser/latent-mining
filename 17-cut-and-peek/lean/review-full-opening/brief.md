# Review brief: the Lean formalisation in `17-cut-and-peek/lean/`

New in the working tree (not committed): the folder `17-cut-and-peek/lean/`, and edits to `17-cut-and-peek/NOTE.md` (subtitle, Summary, Section 6 "Machine check"), `17-cut-and-peek/README.md` and the top-level `README.md` row for experiment 17, which now say that Lemma 1, Theorem 2 and the statements for AND and equality are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib. You cannot run Lean; judge by reading. The Lean kernel has checked the proofs (see `lean/BUILD.log`, `lean/AXIOMS.txt`), so do NOT spend effort on the proof files `Unit.lean`, `Fourier.lean`, `Rows.lean`, `Cube.lean`, `Odd.lean` beyond what a question below asks. What a machine check cannot guarantee is that the definitions and statements say what the note means. That is your job. Assume they are subtly wrong until you have traced them.

Verdict for each question: CONFIRMED / REFUTED / UNSUPPORTED, with file:line evidence.

- Q1. `lean/CutPeek/Spec.lean`, `Row`, `rot`, `CycEq`: for m >= 1, is `ZMod m` the positions modulo m; does `rot s w` rotate the row; is `CycEq` the note's "cyclically equal" (NOTE.md Section 1)? What do these definitions mean for m = 0 (where `ZMod 0` is the integers), and do the theorems exclude m = 0?
- Q2. `Encoding` and `Encoding.row`: does this capture exactly the note's encodings (NOTE.md Section 1: every position is either an additional card, the same for all inputs, or a card determined by one input bit and different for its two values)? Is anything allowed by the Lean definition that the note excludes, or excluded that the note allows (for example several positions controlled by the same bit; a bit controlling no position; all positions additional; arbitrary base cards)? Does it cover the definition of Shinagawa and Nuida as the note paraphrases it (pairs x_i, complement; a known permutation; inserted words)?
- Q3. `IsSCFO`: is "for all inputs x, y: CycEq (row x) (row y) iff f x = f y" the note's definition of an SCFO protocol for f? Is it equivalent to "there are words z0, z1, not cyclically equal, with row x cyclically equal to z_(f x)" when f takes both values, and what happens when f is constant?
- Q4. `patch`, `andFn`, `eqFn`: do they mean what their comments say (in particular, in what order are the three updates applied, and does that matter given that the theorem assumes `i` injective)? Is `andFn n` the AND of n bits and `eqFn n` "all bits equal"?
- Q5. The four statements at the end of `lean/CutPeek/Main.lean`. (a) `seven_force_eighth`: is the hypothesis "the seven rows other than the one at c are pairwise cyclically equal" and the conclusion "the row at c is cyclically equal to each of them", for an arbitrary corner c? Is that Lemma 1 of the note? Could the hypothesis be accidentally unsatisfiable or the conclusion trivial for a reason unrelated to the mathematics? (b) `no_scfo_of_odd_corner`: is the hypothesis the note's "f takes one value at exactly seven of the eight inputs" (note that f is Bool-valued), and is the conclusion "no encoding on m cards is an SCFO protocol for f", for every n and every m >= 1? (c) `no_scfo_and`, `no_scfo_eq`: are the ranges n >= 3 and n >= 4 and "every m >= 1" as the note claims?
- Q6. Non-vacuity. `lean/CutPeek/FiveCard.lean` claims the five-card trick satisfies `IsSCFO` for `andFn 2`. Check by hand that the encoding given there is the five-card trick or at least a valid protocol: compute the four rows and say which are rotations of which. Does this show the definitions are not vacuous in the way that matters?
- Q7. Forbidden constructs. Search the Lean files for `sorry`, `admit`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `unsafe`, `implemented_by`, `opaque`, local notation or instances that could change the meaning of the statements, and `set_option` lines. Does `lean/AXIOMS.txt` list only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound for all five theorems? Do `Main.lean`'s theorems merely apply lemmas proved in the other files (so that nothing is assumed)?
- Q8. Read the first 40 lines of `lean/CutPeek/Fourier.lean` and of `lean/CutPeek/Rows.lean`: is the route the one the note describes (discrete Fourier transform on ZMod m; cards mapped to +1/-1)? Anything surprising, such as an extra hypothesis on m?
- Q9. Do the edited passages of `NOTE.md`, `17-cut-and-peek/README.md`, `lean/README.md` and the top-level row claim more than the Lean files establish? Check: which results are said to be formalised and which not (OR, majority of five, Section 4); "51-line specification"; "about 450 lines of proof"; "Lean 4.33.1"; the statement of what must still be trusted; who wrote and ran the Lean.
